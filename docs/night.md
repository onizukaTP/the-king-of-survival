# Night, moonlight and the torch — approved (D-060, was P-015) and built

Confirmed by the owner (D-048): moving in the dark at night without a torch greatly raises the chance of being injured or ambushed, except on full-moon nights. The injury chance arrives with events (M6) and ambushes with combat (M7). Night, the moon and torch use are designed now and built with M6.

Everything below is approved (D-060) and built with M6 on `dev`. The risk numbers it left open are in `docs/events.md`: in the dark without light, events are twice as likely and *Bad footing* can happen.

## When it is dark

- **Night** runs from **18:30 to 06:00**. That is about sunset to sunrise around Srivilliputhur, which changes little through the year. A new game starts at 06:00 (D-020).
- **The moon** follows a 30-day cycle. Day 1 is a new moon. **Full-moon nights** are the nights of days 14, 15 and 16 of each cycle, then 44–46, and so on.
- **Dark** means night that is not a full-moon night.
- **The top bar** shows a moon icon next to the clock at night: full, or dark.

## Moving in the dark

- **What counts as moving:** any travel leg on the world map, and walking between buildings on a town map, when part of the leg falls in the dark.
- **With the torch:** if Prabu carries a charged torch, it switches on by itself. It uses **10% battery per hour** of dark travel, so the starting 50% lasts 5 hours. With the torch on, the risk is the same as by day.
- **Without light:** with no torch, or a flat one, the risk is raised. The exact chances (injury per hour, ambush per hour) are proposed with M6 and M7. The trip panel shows a warning when a trip will run into the dark without light.
- **Searching and hunting** at night carry no added risk. The torch still makes searching faster, by day and by night (D-037).

## Not included

- **Darkness on the map:** no dimming of the map at night. It could be added later as a visual change only.
- **Animals:** no change to hunting by time of day.
