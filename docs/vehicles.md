## Prototype vehicles (D-031, answers Q10.1)

**Bullock cart, hand cart, cycle with trailer, auto-rickshaw.** All others come after the prototype.

- **Bullock cart** in the prototype: a very rare find (an abandoned cart with a bull). Buying needs trading and catching needs taming, both later.

## Fuel (D-032, answers Q10.2)

People have learned to make **biodiesel**, and it is sold everywhere. It runs vehicles and generators (generators are very rare to find). With no trading in the prototype, biodiesel is **scavenged** there.

## How vehicles are found (D-033, answers Q10.3)

Vehicles and items have drop chances. Some things are found only where they would likely be, and even then very rarely, because scavengers got there first. A cycle can be found whole or built from its parts. *(Parts superseded by D-085: a cycle is only found whole.)*

## Maintenance (D-034, answers Q10.4)

Vehicles wear as they are used: slowly on roads, faster off-road.

## Owner's rulings for M10 (D-085, 2026-11-25)

- **Durability** on every vehicle **except the bullock cart**.
- **Repairs use scrap metal.** Oil joins later as a repair tool.
- **No cycle parts.** A cycle is found whole; the player only needs a **recipe to attach a cart to the cycle** (supersedes "built from its parts" in D-033).
- **The bull's grain and water are deferred**; the bull needs nothing in M10.

## Vehicle numbers — D-087 (approved P-021), built in M10

Walking, for comparison: road 4.5 km/h, cross-country 3 km/h, pack 25 kg free and 50 kg at most (D-019, D-036).

### Speed, cargo and effort

| Vehicle | Road | Cross-country | Cargo | Effort while moving |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| On foot | 4.5 km/h | 3 km/h | Pack only | ×1 |
| Hand cart | 4 km/h | 2 km/h | 100 kg | Food, Water, Rest drain ×1.25 (pushing) |
| Cycle | 12 km/h | 5 km/h | 10 kg (basket) | ×1.25 (pedalling) |
| Cycle with trailer | 10 km/h | 4 km/h | 60 kg | ×1.5 (pedalling a load) |
| Auto-rickshaw | 25 km/h | 8 km/h | 200 kg | ×1 (he sits) |
| Bullock cart | 4 km/h | 3 km/h | 300 kg | ×1 (he rides) |

Why: roads are 27 years without upkeep (potholes, debris, fallen trees), so the cycle and the auto are well below their pre-war speeds, and cross-country is far slower still. The hand cart is a little slower than walking because he is pushing it. The bullock goes at a walking pace but he rides, and it carries the most.

- **Cargo** is separate from the pack: the pack keeps its 25/50 kg limit and its drain penalty, and the cargo adds to it without any penalty.
- **All of them can leave the road**: slower and with more wear (D-034).
- **In a town map**, the vehicle comes with him, and moving between buildings uses its road speed.
- **Parking:** he can get off and leave a vehicle anywhere. It stays there with its cargo for good; it doesn't vanish after 96 hours like dropped items. He uses one vehicle at a time.
- **Night (D-048)** works as on foot: no headlights in the prototype. Faster vehicles mean fewer legs over a distance, so fewer event rolls a kilometre.

### Durability and wear

Durability goes from 0 to 100. Wear is a kilometre, slower on roads and faster off-road (D-034).

| Vehicle | Wear on road | Wear off-road | Road km from 100 to 0 | Found at |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hand cart | 0.2 a km | 0.6 a km | 500 km | 40–80 |
| Cycle | 0.15 a km | 0.5 a km | about 670 km | 30–70 |
| Cycle with trailer | 0.2 a km | 0.6 a km | 500 km | lower of the two it was made from |
| Auto-rickshaw | 0.1 a km | 0.4 a km | 1,000 km | 10–40 |
| Bullock cart | none | none | — | — |

For scale: a round trip from home to Madurai by road (about 160 km) costs the auto 16 durability, the cycle 24 and the hand cart 32.

- **At 25 or below:** a warning in the trip panel, like the meter warnings.
- **At 0 it breaks:** the trip stops at the end of the leg, and it can't move until repaired. Cargo stays with it.
- No random breakdowns: wear alone decides.

### Repairs

| Vehicle | Per scrap metal | Time per scrap metal | Tool |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hand cart | +25 | 20 min | Hunting knife |
| Cycle, cycle with trailer | +20 | 30 min | Hunting knife |
| Auto-rickshaw | +10 | 30 min | Hunting knife |

- A **Repair** action anywhere, like crafting: choose how many scrap metal to use, up to what reaches 100.
- The auto gets the least from scrap metal because an engine needs more than patched metal; oil (later) is the natural fix for that.
- **Engine parts** and the three **cycle parts** (frame, wheel, chain) leave the item list.

### The cycle trailer recipe (D-085)

| Recipe | Inputs | Tool | Time | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cycle trailer | Cycle + hand cart + coir rope ×2 + scrap metal ×1 | Hunting knife | 1 h | Cycle with trailer |

- **Recommended:** attach the hand cart he found, as the owner put it ("attach a cart to the cycle").
- **Alternative:** build the trailer from bamboo ×4, coir rope ×2 and scrap metal ×2 (2 h), with no hand cart needed. This is easier, but the hand cart then has no use beyond itself.
- Both vehicles must be where he is, and the result has the lower durability of the two.

### Fuel: the auto-rickshaw (D-032)

- **Tank:** 10 litres, found with 0–2.
- **Use:** 1 litre a 25 km on road and 1 litre a 12 km off-road. A full tank goes about 250 km on roads.
- **A can of biodiesel** (5 kg) adds 5 litres; he pours it in from the pack or the cargo.
- **Empty tank:** the trip stops at the end of the leg, and he can't move until he refuels.

### Rarity

Tiers: **Uncommon** 3–5% · **Rare** 1–3% · **Very rare** under 1%.

Each search makes one extra roll for vehicles, separate from the item draws:
- The roll falls as the place is stripped, like the other loot (D-047), and Luck raises it as it does every find (D-066).
- A find is his to take there and then, or to leave parked where it is.
- He can own one of each kind.

| Vehicle | Tier | Where (chance per search) |
|---|---|---|
| Hand cart | Uncommon | Railway station 5% (porters' carts) · provision store 4% · ration shop 4% · hardware store 4% · spinning mill / textile shop 3% |
| Cycle | Rare | Garage 3% · independent house 2% · apartment block 2% · railway station 2% (cycle stand) · bus stand 2% |
| Auto-rickshaw | Very rare | Garage 1% · bus stand 1% · petrol bunk 0.7% · railway station 0.7% (auto stand) · NH 744 0.5% |
| Bullock cart | Very rare | Pilavakkal dam 0.5% (farmland) · NH 744 0.5% (an abandoned cart with a bull, D-031) |
| Can of biodiesel (item) | Uncommon to rare | Petrol bunk 12% · garage 8% · NH 744 4% · railway station 2% |

For scale: one full sweep of Srivilliputhur's 13 buildings with fresh stock finds a cycle about 1 time in 9, a hand cart about 1 in 12 (it has no station or hardware store), and an auto about 1 in 60.
